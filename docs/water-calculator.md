# Daily water goal

Updated from the user's formulas on 2026-10-20:
- Weight × 30 mL for the regular selection.
- Weight × 40 or × 50 mL for the two activity/sweating selections.
- (Height in cm + weight in kg) × 10 mL for the alternative formula.

These are explicitly selected tracking estimates. No target exists until Calculate and Save. Incomplete fields never create a denominator. Clearing a target preserves water history. Older weight/age/activity profiles migrate to the weight × 30 formula; the old age/activity algorithm is no longer used.
